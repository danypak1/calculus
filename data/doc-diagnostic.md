# Algebra diagnostic

## Before you begin

This calculus course assumes you're comfortable with a handful of algebra skills — not because you need to be an expert, but because §1.1 (and the sections right after it) will lean on these constantly, especially **plugging expressions into functions** and **expanding things like $(a+h)^2$**.

**This is not a test to pass or fail.** It's a map. Work through it without a calculator, check your answers against the key, and use the "What to review" guide at the end to figure out where to spend 15–20 minutes before you start §1.1. If a topic feels shaky, that's completely normal — that's exactly what this diagnostic is for.

Take your time. There's no clock here.

2. Evaluate: $(5-2)\times(7-4)$
3. Evaluate: $12 \div 3 \times 2$

9. Compute: $-3\times(-4)$
10. Compute: $7-(-2)$

## Section 4: Exponents

11. Evaluate: $2^3$
12. Evaluate: $(-3)^2$
13. Simplify (write as a single power): $x^2\cdot x^3$
14. Evaluate: $5^0$

## Section 5: Expanding and Distributing

15. Expand: $3(x+4)$
16. Expand: $(x+2)^2$
17. Expand, in terms of $x$ and $h$: $(x+h)^2$
18. Expand: $(a+3)(a-3)$

## Section 6: Factoring

19. Factor: $x^2+5x+6$
20. Factor: $x^2-9$
21. Factor out the common factor: $4x+8$

## Section 7: Function Notation and Evaluation

22. If $f(x)=x^2+1$, find $f(3)$.
23. If $f(x)=2x-5$, find $f(-2)$.
24. If $f(x)=x^2+3x$, find $f(a+h)$. (Just substitute and expand — don't simplify further.)
25. If $f(x)=x^2$, find $f(a+h)-f(a)$.

## Section 8: Simplifying Algebraic Fractions

26. Simplify: $\dfrac{2xh+h^2}{h}$ (factor the top, then cancel)
27. Combine into a single fraction: $\dfrac1x-\dfrac12$

## Section 9: Radicals (Square Roots)

28. Simplify: $\sqrt{16}$
29. Simplify (as a simplified radical): $\sqrt{50}$
30. Rationalize the denominator: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$

## Section 10: Solving Linear Equations

33. Solve for $x$: $3(x-1)=12$

## Section 11: Slope and Lines

34. Find the slope of the line through $(1,2)$ and $(4,11)$.
35. Write the equation of a line with slope $2$ that passes through $(0,5)$.

## Section 12: The Coordinate Plane

## Answer key

Check your answers below. For each section, if you missed **one** question, just glance at the fix. If you missed **most or all** of a section, that's your signal to spend a little focused time there before §1.1 — use the "What to review" guide at the very end to know exactly where to look.

### Section 1: Order of Operations

**1.** $3+4\times2^2 = 3+4\times4 = 3+16 = 19$

**2.** $(5-2)\times(7-4) = 3\times3 = 9$

**3.** $12\div3\times2 = 4\times2 = 8$ (left to right, since division and multiplication have equal priority)

### Section 2: Working with Fractions

**4.** $\dfrac13+\dfrac14 = \dfrac{4}{12}+\dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{7}{12}$

**5.** $\dfrac25\times\dfrac34 = \dfrac{6}{20} = \dfrac{3}{10}$

**6.** $\dfrac34\div\dfrac12 = \dfrac34\times\dfrac21 = \dfrac64 = \dfrac32$

**7.** $\dfrac68 = \dfrac34$

### Section 3: Negative Numbers

**8.** $-5+8 = 3$

**9.** $-3\times(-4) = 12$

**10.** $7-(-2) = 7+2 = 9$

### Section 4: Exponents

**11.** $2^3 = 8$

**12.** $(-3)^2 = 9$ (the negative gets squared too)

**13.** $x^2\cdot x^3 = x^5$ (add the exponents)

**14.** $5^0 = 1$ (anything nonzero to the power $0$ is $1$)

### Section 5: Expanding and Distributing

**15.** $3(x+4) = 3x+12$

**16.** $(x+2)^2 = x^2+4x+4$

**17.** $(x+h)^2 = x^2+2xh+h^2$

**18.** $(a+3)(a-3) = a^2-9$

### Section 6: Factoring

**19.** $x^2+5x+6 = (x+2)(x+3)$

**20.** $x^2-9 = (x-3)(x+3)$

**21.** $4x+8 = 4(x+2)$

### Section 7: Function Notation and Evaluation

**22.** $f(3) = 3^2+1 = 9+1 = 10$

**23.** $f(-2) = 2(-2)-5 = -4-5 = -9$

**24.** $f(a+h) = (a+h)^2+3(a+h) = a^2+2ah+h^2+3a+3h$

**25.** $f(a+h)-f(a) = (a^2+2ah+h^2)-a^2 = 2ah+h^2$

### Section 8: Simplifying Algebraic Fractions

**26.** $\dfrac{2xh+h^2}{h} = \dfrac{h(2x+h)}{h} = 2x+h$

**27.** $\dfrac1x-\dfrac12 = \dfrac{2}{2x}-\dfrac{x}{2x} = \dfrac{2-x}{2x}$

### Section 9: Radicals (Square Roots)

**28.** $\sqrt{16} = 4$

**29.** $\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25\times2} = 5\sqrt2$

**30.** $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\times\dfrac{\sqrt2}{\sqrt2} = \dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$

**31.** $\sqrt9+\sqrt4 = 3+2 = 5$

### Section 10: Solving Linear Equations

**32.** $2x+5=13 \Rightarrow 2x=8 \Rightarrow x=4$

**33.** $3(x-1)=12 \Rightarrow 3x-3=12 \Rightarrow 3x=15 \Rightarrow x=5$

### Section 11: Slope and Lines

**35.** $y=2x+5$

## What to review, by section

Not all of these carry equal weight. Some skills are things the course genuinely assumes you already have; others get re-taught early on, so a shaky score there isn't a big red flag.

**High priority — brush up before §1.1 if you missed 2+ in these sections.** These are assumed background and won't be retaught:

| Section | Topic | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Order of operations | Everywhere, constantly |
| 2 | Fractions | Everywhere, constantly |
| 3 | Negative numbers | Everywhere, constantly |
| 6 | Factoring | §1.2, §1.6, §2.3, and most of module 3 |
| 10 | Solving linear equations | Everywhere |
| 11 | Slope and lines | §1.1 itself, and tangent lines throughout the course |

**Lower priority — don't stress if these felt rusty.** The course actually re-teaches these skills, step by step, right at the start:

| Section | Topic | Where it's taught (or re-taught) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Exponents | Reviewed naturally in §1.1, §1.3, and §2.3 |
| 5 | Expanding binomials like $(a+h)^2$ | This is *the* core skill of §1.1 itself — you'll get heavy practice there |
| 7 | Function notation, $f(a+h)$ | Also central to §1.1 — expect a lot of repetition |
| 8 | Simplifying algebraic fractions | Practiced directly in §1.1 and §1.2 |
| 9 | Radicals and rationalizing | Explicitly taught as a technique in §1.1 and §1.2 (the "conjugate trick") |
| 12 | Coordinate plane basics | Used lightly throughout; picks up naturally |

**Bottom line:** if your weak spots are concentrated in Sections 1, 2, 3, 6, 10, or 11, take a short detour before starting — a quick review video or practice set on fractions, factoring, or solving equations will pay off immediately. If your weak spots are in Sections 4, 5, 7, 8, or 9, don't worry — just start §1.1 as planned, since the course builds those skills back up from scratch anyway.
